Questions worth asking.

Do I need my own API keys?

No. Your subscription covers access to every model on the list. Nothing to set up, no separate provider accounts, no per-token billing to watch.

Can I turn models off?

Yes — each column has a toggle. Run all six when a question is worth it, or narrow to two when you just want a quick answer.

Which models exactly?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity and DeepSeek, on their current general-availability versions. When a provider ships a new one, it appears in Crosset without a price change.

What happens to my conversations?

Your prompts are passed to the providers you've switched on so they can answer. Crosset stores your threads so you can search them later, and you can delete any thread permanently from the history view.

Does it work on my phone?

Crosset runs in the browser and adapts to a narrow screen — columns become swipeable rather than side by side. There's nothing to install.
